Privacy policy

This policy explains how Axonic handles information when you use its messaging app and website. For privacy questions, contact the Axonic developer at ivanguachbeltran@gmail.com.

Your device holds your local conversations and downloaded media. Axonic also processes information on servers to operate accounts, deliver messages and files, coordinate calls, and send notifications. Device-first storage does not mean that information never passes through a server.

1. Information we handle and why

  • Account and profile: username, email address, password hash when you use a password, profile picture, profile information, and preferences. We use these to create and secure your account and display your profile to other users.
  • Google sign-in: when you choose this option, we verify a Google identity token and use your verified email and Google account identifier to authenticate or link your Axonic account. Axonic does not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail messages or Google Drive files.
  • Conversations and sharing: message content, photos, videos, documents, voice messages, stickers, and associated filenames or thumbnails are processed to deliver what you send. Chat membership, participant identifiers, message identifiers, timestamps, and delivery/read acknowledgements help route messages and synchronize status. Your local chat database stores conversation history.
  • Contacts and groups: Axonic contact relationships, blocked users, group names, pictures, roles, and membership support private and group conversations. Contact nicknames you create are stored locally on your device.
  • Calls: microphone audio and camera video are used when you make calls or record media. Call signaling and network information help establish connections, sometimes through relay servers. Call history includes participants, type, time, duration, and status; this is separate from the call's audio or video.
  • Device and connection information: installation identifiers, push-notification tokens, platform, device name, app version, online/last-active state, IP addresses, and diagnostic logs help deliver notifications, manage sessions, investigate errors, and prevent abuse.
  • App reliability diagnostics: supported app versions send crash reports to Sentry when crash reporting is configured. Reports can include error types, code stack traces, app/build version, device model, operating-system version, and limited technical state such as connection status. Reports may be queued on your device and sent after reopening the app or reconnecting. We do not intentionally include conversation content, media, contact names, passwords, or access tokens. JavaScript reports are filtered before transmission; native crash reports are produced by the platform SDK and may contain additional technical diagnostics. Screen recordings, screenshots, and session replay are disabled for this integration.
  • Emails and support: we process your email for verification, password recovery, optional offline-message reminders, and support requests. Email providers process the recipient address and email content to deliver these messages.

2. Who receives information

People you communicate with receive the content you send and the profile, group, presence, and message-status information needed for those interactions. They may save, forward, or capture that content.

Service providers process information to operate Axonic: Railway provides backend hosting; DigitalOcean Spaces stores shared media; Google/Firebase supports Google sign-in and Android push delivery; Expo may support push delivery on supported clients; and configured email and call-relay providers support email and calls. Push payloads may contain sender details, message previews, or information needed to retrieve a message, depending on the app version and notification settings.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to investigate abuse and protect users or the service. Providers may process information in countries other than your own.

Reliability service providers

Sentry processes crash diagnostics to help us identify and fix app errors. These reports are sent directly to Sentry rather than through Axonic's messaging server. Expo processes configured app startup and performance measurements. These services receive network requests and therefore process network information needed to receive them. See Sentry's privacy policy and Expo's privacy policy. Diagnostic retention depends on the provider plan and project configuration; crash reports are not a backup of your chats. Contact us with questions or requests about diagnostics.

3. Storage, retention, and security

Account and profile information is retained while your account exists. The server keeps operational metadata for delivery, membership, and calls; it is not a permanent backup of your device's complete chat history. Messages and media may still pass through backend or notification services during delivery.

Shared media is temporarily stored on the server or in object storage. Cleanup jobs remove eligible files after recipients confirm download and a grace period passes, or after an expiry period. Cleanup and delivery-metadata retention depend on the deployed configuration and successful background processing; deletion is not instantaneous.

Local chat history and cached media remain until removed through the app, device storage controls, or uninstalling the app. Photos, videos, or documents exported to your gallery or a selected folder can remain after uninstalling. Deleting a gallery file can make it unavailable in the chat. Recipient copies are not erased by deleting your own copy.

Verification codes expire and are stored as hashes; expired sign-in records are cleaned up in the background. Logs, support records, and provider backups may persist beyond removal from active application storage, subject to operational and legal requirements.

Axonic uses HTTPS and secure WebSocket connections for its production API and realtime connection, and access controls for accounts and media. These protections are not a claim that all content is end-to-end encrypted. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

4. Your choices and device permissions

You can manage profile, notification, discovery, and storage options in the app. Camera, microphone, notification, photo/video, and folder access are used for the features you choose. You can change permissions in your phone's settings; declining access may limit the corresponding feature. Media may remain in app storage when export permission or a destination folder is unavailable.

The website uses browser storage for login/session information and app functionality. Treat shared devices carefully and sign out when finished. Google sign-in and other third-party services have their own privacy policies.

5. Delete your Axonic account or request your data

Use the account-deletion option in the app, or email ivanguachbeltran@gmail.com with an Axonic account deletion request. You do not need to reinstall the app to make a request. Include your Axonic username and send the request from your registered email where possible. Never send your password or verification code.

We may ask for information needed to verify ownership. Account deletion removes your account and associated active account records. It does not remotely erase conversations or media already saved by recipients, files exported to phone storage, or records that must be retained for security or legal obligations. Temporary files and residual provider copies may require subsequent cleanup.

You can also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. The rights available to you depend on your location; we will handle requests under applicable requirements.

6. Children and policy changes

If you believe a child has provided information without appropriate authorization, contact us so we can investigate and address it. We may update this policy as Axonic changes. The date above identifies the current version; material changes will be communicated as required.
